The five

Details, interest, retention, vision, order.

Five plain words. Each one is a piece of the evening you currently spend on preparation. One of them ships today and the other four are in build, labelled honestly wherever they appear.

Available now

Details

A deal turns on a detail. Usually one you did not check.

Type an address. Every detail worth having comes back in eight sections, and every figure arrives carrying its source and the number of transactions behind it.

What we cannot source is named as missing rather than quietly filled in. So you walk into the appointment holding all of it, and holding nothing you cannot explain.

Figures load from captured output. Nothing on this page is invented, so the slots below stay empty until there is a real number to put in them.

  • 01Property details

    Tenure, floor area, completion year and unit mix, read from the registry.

  • 02Market prices

    Transactions in the project and around it, each median stamped with the sales behind it.

  • 03Costs and financing

    Stamp duties, the cash and CPF split, and instalments your client can check line by line.

  • 04Schools

    Inside the one and two kilometre bands, measured from MOE and OneMap data.

  • 05Transport

    Stations, bus connections and drive times, drawn from LTA records.

  • 06Amenities

    What sits within walking distance, located rather than described.

  • 07Area outlook

    Planning decisions on record for the area, linked back to the primary document.

  • 08Sources and gaps

    Every source cited, and every figure we could not find written down as missing.

Interest

Interest is a fact. Stop reading it off a face.

Send the deck as a link instead of a file. You see which sections he opened, how long he stayed in each one, what he forwarded, and what he never went back to.

He came in talking about schools. He spent fourteen of his nineteen minutes on financing and forwarded it twice. Open Monday with the section he chose, not the one you assumed.

One link per client. Revocable. Always reading current data, so a deck sent in March does not quote March prices in June.

Retention

You do not lose clients to competitors. You lose them to silence.

BASIS reads property news and the official record every day and goes to the primary document for Parliament and ministry announcements, not to somebody's summary of it.

Then it does the hour you never have. It works out which of your own clients each change actually touches, groups them, and drafts the message for each group. The client who went quiet in March hears from you the morning something moves that affects him. You read it, change what you want, and send.

  • Primary record, not a summary

    Parliament and ministry releases read at source and linked back to the document.

  • Sorted by client, not by topic

    The question it answers is who this hits, not what happened today.

  • Drafted, never sent without you

    A message per group, waiting for your edit. Nothing leaves under your name until you send it.

  • A reason to call

    Contact that is useful to the person receiving it, which is the only kind that keeps working.

Vision

Most buyers cannot picture it. So hand them the picture.

An empty room is a hard sell, because the buyer is standing in it doing the imagining alone and on the spot. Furnish it in the photograph instead, with every piece identified and the shops that sell it.

Then let them walk the unit in 3D, from a phone, on the train, at midnight. The ones who turn up have already decided the layout works. Fewer wasted viewings, on both sides.

  • Furnished from a photograph

    Upload a bare room, get a styled one back.

  • Pieces identified, with sources

    Not decoration. A list your client can actually buy from.

  • The unit in 3D

    Built from the floor plan rather than from a camera rig you have to book.

  • Walkable from anywhere

    No app, no appointment, no travel time spent on a maybe.

In build

Order

Both meanings. Bookings taken, and a week that arranges itself.

A site under your own name, carrying your listings and your CEA registration number, taking consultations while you are standing in someone else's living room.

You are unreachable for most of the working day. This is the part of you that is not. Bookings arrive in one place, and the week is arranged before you get back to your phone.

  • A site under your name

    Your listings, your registration number, no vendor's logo on it.

  • Consultations booked without you

    A calendar that keeps working through a viewing.

  • One place for the day

    Enquiries and bookings in order rather than across four apps.
