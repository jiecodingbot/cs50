What we do

We do the preparation. You do the part that needs a person.

BASIS is a working surface built for licensed agents in Singapore. It reads the public record every day, does the groundwork you would otherwise do by hand at night, and hands it back in a form you can put straight in front of a client.

It does not price your listing for you and it does not talk to your client for you. Those are yours. Everything in between is ours.

01

Sourced, never estimated

Every figure traces back to URA, HDB, MOE, LTA or OneMap, and arrives carrying its sample size. A median built from two transactions says two transactions, on the slide, in front of your client.

02

Gaps are named, not filled

Anything we cannot source is written down as missing rather than quietly guessed. You will never have to defend a number you cannot explain.

03

It goes out under your name

Your name, your agency and your CEA registration number sit on the output. The client sees your work, not a vendor's logo.

04

Honest about what is live

One part of this ships today. The rest is in build and labelled as such on this page. We will tell you the day each one opens and we will not bill you for it before then.
