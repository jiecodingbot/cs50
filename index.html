Details

A deal turns on a detail. Usually one you did not check.

Type an address. Every detail worth having comes back in eight sections, each figure carrying its source and the number of transactions behind it.

What we cannot source is named as missing rather than quietly filled in.

Figures load from captured output. The slots stay empty until there is a real number to put in them.

  • 01Property details

    Tenure, floor area, completion year and unit mix, read from the registry.

  • 02Market prices

    Transactions in the project and around it, each median stamped with the sales behind it.

  • 03Costs and financing

    Stamp duties, the cash and CPF split, and instalments your client can check line by line.

  • 04Schools

    Inside the one and two kilometre bands, from MOE and OneMap data.

  • 05Transport

    Stations, bus connections and drive times, from LTA records.

  • 06Amenities

    What sits within walking distance, located rather than described.

  • 07Area outlook

    Planning decisions on record, linked back to the primary document.

  • 08Sources and gaps

    Every source cited, and every figure we could not find named as missing.

Interest is a fact. Stop reading it off a face.

Send the deck as a link instead of a file. You see which sections he opened, how long he stayed, what he forwarded, and what he never went back to.

He came in talking about schools. He spent fourteen of his nineteen minutes on financing. Open Monday with the section he chose.

03

In build

Retention

You do not lose clients to competitors. You lose them to silence.

BASIS reads the news and the official record every day, works out which of your clients each change actually touches, groups them, and drafts the message.

The client who went quiet in March hears from you the morning something moves that affects him.

  • Primary record, not a summary

    Parliament and ministry releases read at source.

  • Sorted by client, not by topic

    The question it answers is who this hits.

  • Drafted, never sent without you

    Nothing leaves under your name until you send it.

04

In build

Vision

Most buyers cannot picture it. So hand them the picture.

Furnish the empty room in the photograph, with every piece identified and the shops that sell it.

Then let them walk the unit in 3D, from a phone, at midnight. The ones who turn up have already decided the layout works.

  • Furnished from a photograph

    Upload a bare room, get a styled one back.

  • Pieces identified, with sources

    A list your client can actually buy from.

  • The unit in 3D

    Built from the floor plan, not a camera rig you book.

In build

Order

Both meanings. Bookings taken, and a week that arranges itself.

A site under your own name, carrying your listings and your CEA registration number, taking consultations while you are in someone else's living room.

You are unreachable for most of the working day. This is the part of you that is not.

  • A site under your name

    Your listings, your registration number, no vendor's logo.

  • Booked without you

    A calendar that keeps working through a viewing.

  • One place for the day

    Enquiries and bookings in order, not across four apps.
